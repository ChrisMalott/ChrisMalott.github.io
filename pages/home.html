Why convert in the browser?

Most online converters upload your video to a server, process it there and make you download it again. That is slow for large files and means a stranger's computer holds a copy of your footage. Here, the conversion happens inside your browser tab: nothing is uploaded, so it works for private videos and keeps working on slow connections once the engine has loaded.

What you need

A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or phone. The converter engine is about 31 MB and downloads once, the first time you press Convert; after that your browser caches it. Files up to {{maxFileMB}} MB are supported. Conversions use your device's processor, so a laptop or desktop is noticeably faster than a phone for long videos.
