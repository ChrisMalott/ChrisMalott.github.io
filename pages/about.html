About {{siteName}}

{{siteName}} is a small collection of free, single-purpose media converters. Each page does one job – like turning an MP4 into a GIF or a MOV into an MP4 – with sensible defaults and only the settings that matter.

How it works

The converters use FFmpeg, compiled to WebAssembly by the open-source ffmpeg.wasm project. When you press Convert, your browser downloads the FFmpeg engine from this site and runs it locally in a background worker. Your file is read straight from your disk into that worker, converted, and handed back to you as a download. It is never sent over the network.

Limits

Because everything happens in your browser's memory, files are limited to {{maxFileMB}} MB, and speed depends on your device. The engine runs on a single processor core so that the site works in every browser without special server configuration.

Free to use

The site is supported by advertising. There are no accounts, watermarks or daily limits. See the privacy policy for details, and the open-source credits for the software that makes it possible.
